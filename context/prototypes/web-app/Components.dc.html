Design, not built Components: a reference sheet, not a screen of the app. Every artboard copies its parts from here.

Messaging Explorer components

One system for every screen: Material 3 tokens from the app theme, one accent, the error colour only for what destroys or fails. Each group names its parts and says when to use them. Sample data is invented.

Colour tokens

Use a token by name, never a hex value. Containers carry their own "on" colour for text; scrim dims the screen behind a dialog; shadow lifts menus and dialogs.

Inline messages

Use inside a panel or dialog, next to what it is about, when the text must stay on screen. Each kind has its own icon; the first words say what happened.

Info. Operations are at-least-once: a message that was sent and could not be removed from its source is reported as a possible duplicate.

Warning. A resubmit from this subscription publishes to the topic order-events, so every subscription receives the message again.

Error. The queue was not saved: the broker refused a lock duration above 5 minutes. Nothing was changed.

Success. 37 messages were resubmitted to orders. None failed, none may be duplicated.

Inline message: info, warning, error, success. No border, no stripe: the container colour, the icon and the first word.

Empty, loading and error states

Use one of the three wherever content would be, and never one for another: empty says what to do next, loading says what is being read, error says what failed and offers Retry.

No queues in this namespace

The namespace answered and holds no queue.

Loading state: spinner, what is being read and from where, placeholders in the shape of the content

Error state: what failed, what that means for the data, Retry
