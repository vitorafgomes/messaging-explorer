# Core: RabbitMQ message operations

One topic of [[pages/services/core|Core]]; the map lists the others. The provider itself, its two transports and its mapping are on [[pages/services/core/rabbitmq-and-legacy]].

- **A send is confirmed, and where it goes is decided by the broker's names (2026-11-16).** A send publishes on a channel of its own with `mandatory` and a publisher confirm (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#SendConfirmed`): to the queue of that name through the default exchange when there is one, whatever the subject (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQSendTests.cs#A_send_to_a_queue_goes_through_the_default_exchange_with_mandatory_and_waits_for_the_confirm_whatever_its_subject`), otherwise to the exchange of that name with the subject as routing key (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#IsQueue`). A message the broker returned, did not accept or did not confirm in time, and a name that is neither, fail with a sentence that says which, as `InvalidEntityConfigurationException` from the single send and as the failure of that index in a batch (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQSendTests.cs#In_a_batch_a_message_the_broker_returns_fails_with_its_reason_and_the_ones_after_it_are_not_sent`). Before, the subject alone chose exchange or queue and nothing was confirmed, so a send to a fanout exchange without a subject, or to a queue with one, was dropped and reported as sent. A queue and an exchange of one name: the queue wins.
- **A peek the broker refuses is the neutral read failure, and a stream is never asked (2026-11-16).** A stream answers a get by closing the whole connection, so the queue's type is read from the management API first and a stream is refused there, on a peek and on the three bulk calls (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#IsStreamAsync`, `src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQPeekFailureTests.cs#A_peek_of_a_stream_is_refused_before_the_broker_is_asked_because_a_stream_closes_the_connection_on_a_get`); when the management API cannot be reached the type is unknown and the broker decides. A refusal that closes only the channel, as for a queue exclusive to another connection, leaves the provider as `MessageReadFailedException` with a cause this application wrote (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQPeekFailureTests.cs#A_peek_the_broker_refuses_is_the_neutral_read_failure_with_a_cause_and_nothing_of_the_broker_text`, `src/ServiceBusExplorer.IntegrationTests/RabbitMQProviderDefectTests.cs#A_peek_of_a_stream_or_of_a_queue_exclusive_to_another_connection_says_why_and_a_later_peek_still_works`). A receive and a purge ask the type too: a receive on a stream is the same read failure, and a purge is refused with "A stream cannot be purged: it keeps its messages until its retention removes them." (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQPeekFailureTests.cs#A_purge_of_a_stream_is_refused_before_the_broker_is_asked_and_says_a_stream_cannot_be_purged`, `src/ServiceBusExplorer.IntegrationTests/RabbitMQProviderDefectTests.cs#A_receive_and_a_purge_on_a_stream_are_refused_with_a_reason_and_the_connection_stays_open`). A delete of every message, which is a purge, refuses a stream the same way (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQPeekFailureTests.cs#A_delete_of_every_message_of_a_stream_is_refused_before_the_broker_is_asked_as_a_purge_is`). A search is refused as not supported, declared by `SupportsMessageSearch` false, never answered as an empty result (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQPeekFailureTests.cs#A_search_is_refused_as_not_supported_never_answered_as_an_empty_result`).
- **Delete, move and Edit & Resubmit are offered in the screen since 2026-11-16:** `SupportsBulkMessageOperations` is true (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProviderCapabilities.cs#SupportsBulkMessageOperations`). A message is still a position, declared by `SupportsSequenceNumbers` false ([[pages/services/web-app/bulk-by-position]]).
- **The resubmit sends first and removes after (2026-11-15).** `ResubmitDeadLetterMessagesAsync` holds the dead-letter messages unacknowledged on its own channel, publishes each chosen one to its queue through the default exchange with `mandatory` and a publisher confirm, and acknowledges it only once the broker confirmed; the rest are requeued in their order (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#TransferConfirmed`). A dead-letter queue with a consumer is refused, since a position may have shifted. A message the broker refuses or does not confirm stays in the dead-letter queue and is reported; a confirm that times out, or a removal that fails after the send, is reported as a possible duplicate. A connection that drops while waiting for the confirm is a possible duplicate; a channel the broker closed with 403 or 406 is a refusal, except a 406 for an expired delivery (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#IsBrokerRefusal`). Any outcome other than a clean confirm stops the batch and reports the positions left as not sent, so a late answer never lands on another message. A success counts only once the broker is shown to have processed its removal, by a graceful close or by a channel it closed for a 403 or 406 refusal, which it answers after the frames before it; otherwise it is a possible duplicate (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#RemovalsSettled`). A close that throws, whatever the exception, proves nothing and is that same possible duplicate (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQResubmitFailureTests.cs#A_close_that_times_out_leaves_the_removal_unproven_and_reports_the_sent_message_as_a_possible_duplicate`). Every possible duplicate carries the `PossibleDuplicate` flag, which the web app reads to keep a message out of a retry, and its text says "may be duplicated", the second guard the web app keeps (`web-app/src/app/core/services/bulk-operations.service.ts#isPossibleDuplicate`); what the messages dialog does with a position is on [[pages/services/web-app/bulk-by-position]]. A publish the broker returned as unroutable, as to a queue that no longer exists, stays in the dead-letter queue (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.IntegrationTests/RabbitMQProviderTests.cs#A_resubmit_to_a_queue_that_no_longer_exists_is_returned_by_the_broker_and_the_message_stays_in_the_dead_letter_queue`). The single resubmit runs the same path with its one position (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#ResubmitDeadLetterMessageAsync`); the web app's detail pane calls the batch route with one position on RabbitMQ, so a failure shows its reason, and the automatic resubmit calls the batch. A message under a binding goes back to its queue, not through the exchange. Pinned by `src/ServiceBusExplorer.IntegrationTests/RabbitMQProviderTests.cs#A_resubmit_of_dead_letter_position_2_sends_only_that_message_to_the_queue_and_keeps_the_others_in_their_order` and `src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQResubmitFailureTests.cs#A_message_that_was_sent_and_could_not_be_removed_is_reported_as_a_possible_duplicate_never_as_a_success`.
- **Delete and move run on the resubmit's path (2026-11-16).** `DeleteMessagesAsync` and `MoveMessagesAsync` call the same member as the resubmit (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#TransferConfirmed`): the messages of the source up to the last chosen position are held unacknowledged on a channel of their own; a move publishes each chosen one to the target queue through the default exchange with `mandatory` and a publisher confirm and acknowledges it only once the broker confirmed (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_move_sends_the_chosen_message_to_the_target_before_it_removes_it_and_puts_the_others_back`); a delete publishes nothing and only acknowledges (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_delete_removes_only_the_chosen_position_sends_nothing_and_puts_the_others_back`); the others go back in their order. A source with a consumer, or one that does not exist, is refused before anything is read (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_queue_with_a_consumer_is_refused_and_nothing_is_read`). A target that does not exist returns the message, which stays in the source (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.IntegrationTests/RabbitMQProviderDefectTests.cs#A_move_to_a_missing_queue_reports_the_failure_and_the_message_stays_in_the_source`). The texts are the resubmit's with "Not moved" or "Not deleted" and "the queue" or "the dead-letter queue"; a delete whose removal the broker did not prove says "The removal from the queue was not confirmed. The message may still be there." and is a failure, never a success (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_delete_whose_channel_close_is_not_answered_is_reported_as_unconfirmed_never_as_a_success`). The caller's token stops the batch between two messages (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_move_cancelled_during_the_batch_sends_no_more_and_reports_the_positions_left`), and a move reads the queue of the subscription it was given (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_move_from_a_subscription_reads_the_queue_of_that_subscription_and_its_dead_letter_queue`). The publish and its confirm run on a second channel, so a publish the broker refuses closes that one and the held messages are requeued by the channel that holds them instead of being returned by its death, which a quorum queue counts as a delivery (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_publish_the_broker_refuses_closes_only_the_publishing_channel_and_the_held_messages_are_requeued_by_the_one_that_holds_them`); this holds for the resubmit too. A position beyond 1000, the most a peek shows, is refused before anything is read, so the messages held are bounded (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_position_beyond_the_1000_a_peek_can_show_is_refused_and_no_message_is_read_or_held`), and a read that fails midway fails every position with nothing sent or removed (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core.Tests/Providers/RabbitMQBulkDeleteAndMoveFailureTests.cs#A_read_that_fails_midway_fails_every_position_sends_and_removes_nothing_and_requeues_what_was_held`). `all` maps to `QueuePurge` (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.Core/Providers/RabbitMQ/RabbitMQProvider.cs#DeleteMessagesAsync`). A position is still not an identity: a message removed by someone else between the peek and the call shifts the ones behind it (`src/ServiceBusExplorer.IntegrationTests/RabbitMQProviderDefectTests.cs#Defect_delete_by_position_removes_whichever_message_is_at_that_position_now`).
